Alkhidmat MUN is dedicated to fostering leadership and diplomatic skills among youth through engaging Model United Nations conferences. Participants collaborate, debate, and develop solutions to global issues, enhancing their public speaking and critical thinking abilities. Our event brings together students from diverse backgrounds, encouraging teamwork and cultural understanding. Join us to experience a platform that empowers the next generation of leaders and changemakers.

Code Of Conduct

  1. All delegates must maintain decorum and respect towards chairs, staff, and fellow participants.
  2. Use of discriminatory or offensive language will not be tolerated.
  3. Delegates must adhere to formal dress code unless informed otherwise.
  4. Plagiarism in position papers or speeches is strictly prohibited.
  5. Mobile phones must be on silent mode during sessions; use only during breaks.
  6. Consumption of drugs or alcohol at the event premises will result in immediate expulsion.
  7. Delegates are expected to be punctual for all sessions and events.
  8. Organizers reserve the right to take disciplinary action for any misconduct.
