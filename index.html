Code Of Conduct

  • All delegates must maintain decorum and respect towards chairs, staff, and fellow participants.
  • Use of discriminatory or offensive language will not be tolerated.
  • Delegates must adhere to formal dress code unless informed otherwise.
  • Plagiarism in position papers or speeches is strictly prohibited.
  • Mobile phones must be on silent mode during sessions; use only during breaks.
  • Consumption of drugs or alcohol at the event premises will result in immediate expulsion.
  • Delegates are expected to be punctual for all sessions and events.
  • Organizers reserve the right to take disciplinary action for any misconduct.
